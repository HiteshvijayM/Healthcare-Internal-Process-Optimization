## 2. Logging Rules

- Record date, owner, status, impacted files, summary, and validation evidence.
- Keep next steps current.

- Planned: defined but not started
- In Progress: actively being implemented
- Implemented: completed with validation evidence

## 7. Resolved decisions

The nine questions surfaced by the CHG-019 specification pass were answered under CHG-020. Four were settled decisively by evidence already in the repository rather than by preference, which is recorded per item below. Full reasoning is in `openspec/changes/administrative-workflow-assistant/design.md` under "Resolved Decisions".

| # | Question | Decision | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What does F3 backfill read from? | Two tiers: the processed-case history indexed on patient reference, plus a declared prior-records fixture for patient references with no earlier processed case. | **Evidence.** CASE-014 backfills from the CASE-009 record for the same patient reference, which the history tier serves; CASE-002, CASE-011 and CASE-017 cite stores that do not exist as fixtures, which the fixture tier serves. Rescoping was rejected — it would strand acceptance scenario 2 and four dataset cases. |
| 2 | What counts as a critical-condition signal? | Detection recast as **declared** criticality: markers the source or its qualified author has asserted, matched against a clinician-approved marker list, never inferred. Unrecognised or ambiguous markers fail closed to human review. | **Evidence.** CASE-008's source carries an explicit critical result flag and its answer key records that interpreting that flag is a Sev 0 failure. Detection is therefore string matching, not clinical reasoning — clinician sign-off narrows from authoring a taxonomy to approving a list. |
| 3 | Is Legal a release clearance gate or approval-only? | Approval-only. No specification change. | F16, F17 and F18 name only clinical and financial clearance as gates. |
| 4 | Is clinical-then-financial ordering a real business rule? | Yes — strict ordering retained. No specification change. | Financial clearance ahead of clinical clearance would authorise spend against a procedure not yet clinically approved. The conservative and correct readings coincide. |
| 5 | Which clinical authority receives which escalation? | The single registered Clinical Authority role; no attribute-keyed routing table. A deputy or on-call holder is added so the P4 30-minute target stays achievable. | **Evidence.** Harness §4.1 registers one Clinical Authority role and the answer key names one escalation target. |
| 6 | How is the P8 retention gap resolved? | Keep the 90-day floor, explicitly labelled a synthetic-data project value and not a production retention posture.
